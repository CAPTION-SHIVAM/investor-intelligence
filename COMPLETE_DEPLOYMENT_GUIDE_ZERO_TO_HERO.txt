6. STEP 5: CONNECT YOUR CUSTOM DOMAIN (GODADDY / NAMECHEAP / HOSTINGER / CLOUDFLARE)
================================================================================
If you own a custom domain (e.g. yourname.in, investoriq.in, investorintelligence.com):

1. Go to your project on Vercel:
   https://vercel.com/dashboard -> Select your project -> Click "Settings" -> "Domains".

2. In the domain input box, type your domain name (e.g. yourdomain.com or yourdomain.in)
   and click "Add".

3. Vercel will show you the exact DNS records to configure. Usually:
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
   Type:   A Record
   Name:   @ (or leave blank depending on registrar)
   Value:  76.76.21.21

   Type:   CNAME Record
   Name:   www
   Value:  cname.vercel-dns.com
   -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

4. Log in to your Domain Registrar (GoDaddy, Namecheap, Hostinger, or Cloudflare):
   - Go to "DNS Management" / "DNS Records".
   - Add the A Record: Host = @, Points to = 76.76.21.21 (TTL: 1 Hour / Automatic).
   - Add the CNAME Record: Host = www, Points to = cname.vercel-dns.com (TTL: 1 Hour).

5. Back on Vercel, the domain status will turn green with a checkmark "Valid Configuration".
   Vercel automatically issues and renews a FREE HTTPS/SSL Certificate for your domain!

Your platform is now officially live on your custom domain (https://yourdomain.in)!
